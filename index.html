Research interests

Brain decoding

The topic I'm aiming for the most is working with brain decoding techniques, which consist of applying machine learning algorithms to brain data (fMRI, EEG, MEG) in order to understand and interpret what a person is thinking or dreaming.

Brain decoding fascinates me from both a pure intellectual curiosity perspective and from a more tangible one, as there are many fields that benefit from this kind of research: from the more obvious goal of gaining a better understanding of how the human brain works, to the more practical application of brain-computer interfaces, that amongst many other advantages, allow people with disabilities to live a better life.

Papers that shaped my interest

Shen, Horikawa, Majima & Kamitani · PLoS Comput. Biol. 2019

Deep image reconstruction from human brain activity

The work that has brought me to this interest, because it was the first one I encountered that was actually able to reconstruct from the brain activity images of what people were seeing or even imagining in their minds.

Kriegeskorte & Kievit · Trends Cogn. Sci. 2013

Representational geometry: integrating cognition, computation, and the brain

Kriegeskorte's work on representational similarity analysis really changed how comparisons between artificial and biological neural networks are performed. I really like this study for the simplicity and straightforwardness of the method he introduced, while being really effective and powerful, making easy and fast the comparison between all types of representation systems.
